About Us & Our Vision

AtmosVision was built to bridge the gap between complex meteorological data and everyday life. Our vision is to provide a platform where planning your day is as simple as talking to an AI. We believe weather forecasting should be proactive, conversational, and highly accurate.

AtmosVision Disaster Mesh Routing

Severe weather events frequently disrupt cellular infrastructure. The AtmosVision Mesh Protocol is a fail-safe localized network that allows offline devices to bounce SOS telemetry device-to-device until an active rescue hub is reached.

  • Active Routing Logic: Low-latency ad-hoc packet forwarding utilizing simulated hardware-accelerated nodes.
  • Development Roadmap: Autonomous UAV drone repeater deployment and AI-driven triage prioritization for rescue dispatch.
